# Personality, Opinions & Values – Shahrina Khan

## Who I Am
I am Shahrina Khan, a BSCS student at UMT Sialkot in my seventh semester. I see myself as a continuous learner who enjoys discovering new things and improving my knowledge. I like exploring different technologies, understanding how they work, and turning ideas into useful projects.

I prefer practical learning. Reading about a tool or concept is useful, but I understand it better when I implement small examples myself. I also believe that technical errors are a normal part of learning — understanding error messages and fixing problems helps me grow as a developer.

## My Hobbies

Cooking is one of my favourite hobbies. I love preparing food and find it a creative and practical activity outside of my academic and technical work.

**Exploring New Technology**  
I also enjoy exploring new technology. I like discovering new tools, frameworks, and software that can make development easier or offer better ways to solve problems. Technology keeps changing, so staying curious helps me keep learning.

**Travel**  
Travel is another thing I love. I enjoy travelling and have travelled to the northern areas of Pakistan. Exploring new places, especially the mountains and natural beauty of the north, is something I really enjoy.

**Continuous Learning**  
Learning new things in general is important to me. Whether it is through university courses, documentation, online resources, or building projects, I enjoy gaining knowledge in areas I have not explored before.

## My Approach to Technology
I am interested in the practical side of technology — how applications are designed, developed, and actually used by people. I find it interesting that a simple idea can become a working application through programming, logical thinking, and problem-solving.

I am especially interested in combining traditional software development with artificial intelligence. Building full-stack applications that include frontend, backend, databases, APIs, and AI features is something I enjoy and want to keep improving at.

I have worked on generative AI chatbots, n8n automation projects, and RAG-based systems. These experiences have strengthened my interest in applied AI and agentic applications.

## Values & Mindset
- Continuous learning is essential in computer science because tools and frameworks keep evolving.
- Practical implementation is more valuable than only theoretical knowledge.
- Building real projects (even small ones) is the best way to understand technology.
- I prefer working end-to-end — from frontend interfaces to backend services, databases, and AI integration.
- I value clear, useful solutions that solve real problems rather than just theoretical experiments.

## Current Focus
At this stage of my degree, I am focused on improving my technical skills through software projects, exploring new technologies, and understanding how different computing concepts can be applied to real-world problems. I am particularly interested in projects that combine software development with artificial intelligence and research-oriented functionality.

## What I Prefer Not to Claim
I do not claim professional-level expertise in every technology I have explored. Many of my skills are at the level of academic projects, personal experiments, and continuous learning. I prefer to be honest about what I have built and what I am still improving.
